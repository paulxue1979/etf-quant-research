## Semantics and Evidence

`PEAK_DRAWDOWN` is the drawdown of the selected close from the highest real
close observed through the evaluation date. It is no-lookahead, respects RAW
versus ADJUSTED price field, and is available only as a `VALUE`/`DEEP_VALUE`
Regime entry condition. Its threshold is user-configurable and may be joined
with the existing condition tree through OR. Existing exit logic and topology
are unchanged.

The Official OOS history requirement is `ALL_TIME_TO_DATE` for active Peak
Drawdown conditions. Indicator context may predate IS/OOS, while official
returns, trades, equity and metrics remain within the frozen OOS evaluation
range. Browser UAT used QQQ real-price history from 1999-03-10 with metrics
limited to 2024-01-02 through 2025-12-31; source was the existing cache.

Freeze POST now returns structured JSON for nested immutable provenance after
the transaction commits. Browser UAT observed HTTP 200, immediate Frozen UI,
reload persistence, one Selection/Freeze/OOS record per protocol, completed
Official OOS, and a readable Research View. Domain identity and hashes remain
unchanged.
